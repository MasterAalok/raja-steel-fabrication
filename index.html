About Raja Steel Fabrication

Craftsmanship in Steel & Iron

We are a family-run welding and fabrication workshop delivering durable metal solutions for homes, shops and small industries. Every piece we build is welded by hand, finished with care, and made to outlast the weather.

  • Custom designs tailored to your space
  • On-time delivery and clean installation
